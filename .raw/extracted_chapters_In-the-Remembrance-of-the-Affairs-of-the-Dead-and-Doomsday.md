## Chapter 1: Proofs of the pains of death and the condition of dying people

### Quranic Verses on the Stupor of Death
Allah, glory to Him, describes the severity of the stupor of death in the Qur'an:
* *"And the stupor of death comes in truth..."* (Surah Qaf, 50:19)
* *"...and if you could but see when the wrongdoers are in the agonies of death..."* (Surah Al-An'am, 6:93)
* *"...and when the soul reaches the throat..."* (Surah Al-Waqi'ah, 56:83)

### Prophetic Hadiths on the Agonies of Death
**Hadith 20:**
> Lady Aisha (may Allah be pleased with her) reported that during the final illness of Prophet Muhammad (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him), there was a pot of water beside him. He kept dipping his hands into the water and wiping his face with them, saying:
> 
> *"There is no god but Allah. Truly, death has its agonies (pains)."*
> 
> Then he raised his hand and kept saying:
> 
> *"To the Supreme Companion (or the highest company)..."* until he passed away and his hand fell.
> 
> *(Reported by Sahih al-Bukhari)*

## Chapter 2: Death expiates Muslim's sins

### Illness and Pain as an Expiation of Sins
**Hadith 21:**
> Prophet Muhammad (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) said:
> 
> *"No pain or sickness afflicts a Muslim, whether it be a minor prick of a thorn or something more serious, except that Allah expiates his sins thereby, just as a tree sheds its leaves."*
> 
> *(Reported by Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim)*

**Hadith 22:**
> On the authority of Abu Hurairah, the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) said:
> 
> *"If Allah wants to do good to somebody, He afflicts him with trials."*
> 
> *(Reported in Al-Muwatta and Sahih al-Bukhari)*

## Chapter 3: Man should have a favorable opinion of Allah at his death (Fearing Allah, glory to Him)

### Favorable Opinion of Allah
**Hadith 23:**
> On the authority of Jabir ibn Abdullah, the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) said three days before his death:
> 
> *"None of you should die except having a good (favorable) opinion of Allah."*
> 
> *(Reported by Sahih Muslim)*

### Mixing Hope and Fear at Death
**Hadith 24:**
> On the authority of Anas ibn Malik, the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) visited a young man who was close to death and asked him:
> 
> *"How do you feel?"*
> 
> The young man replied:
> 
> *"O Messenger of Allah! I have hope in Allah's mercy, yet I fear my sins."*
> 
> The Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) said:
> 
> *"These two feelings (hope and fear) do not gather in a believer's heart in such circumstances, but Allah will grant him what he hopes for and protect him from what he fears."*
> 
> *(Reported by Ibn Majah and Ibn Abi Al-Dunya, and verified as Hasan by At-Tirmidhi)*
